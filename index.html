#01 Chola Heritage & Ancient Fossils

Ariyalur

Gangaikonda Cholapuram Temple
OVERVIEW District Information

Renowned for the majestic Gangaikonda Cholapuram temple built by Rajendra Chola I to commemorate his northern victory, this UNESCO World Heritage Site showcases supreme stone architecture. Ariyalur is also globally celebrated by geologists for its 65-million-year-old marine fossil beds and rich limestone reserves.

View on Google Maps
#02 UNESCO World Heritage & Coastal Art

Chengalpattu

Mahabalipuram Shore Temple
OVERVIEW District Information

Home to the world-renowned UNESCO monuments of Mahabalipuram, including the 7th-century Shore Temple, Krishna's Butter Ball, and monolithic rock-cut cave sanctuaries. The district also houses the tranquil Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary, one of the oldest waterbird sanctuaries in India.

View on Google Maps
#03 Capital Metropolis & Cultural Gateway

Chennai

Marina Beach & Kapaleeshwarar
OVERVIEW District Information

The historic gateway to South India, blending colonial heritage with deep-rooted Carnatic traditions. Famous for Marina Beach—one of the world's longest natural urban beaches—as well as the historic 7th-century Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Fort St. George, and vibrant culinary culture.

View on Google Maps
#04 Manchester of South India & Hill Gateways

Coimbatore

Marudhamalai & Western Ghats
OVERVIEW District Information

Flanked by the misty Nilgiri foothills, Coimbatore is celebrated for its pleasant climate, entrepreneurship, and legendary Kongu hospitality. Known for the hill shrine of Marudhamalai, the sweet waters of Siruvani River, and serving as the primary launchpad to Ooty and Valparai.

View on Google Maps
#05 Mangroves & Cosmic Dance

Cuddalore

Pichavaram Mangrove Forests
OVERVIEW District Information

Celebrated for the awe-inspiring Pichavaram mangrove forest, one of the largest in the world, where tranquil boat channels weave through dense green canopies. It is also home to the cosmic Nataraja Temple at Chidambaram and the scenic Silver Beach along the Bay of Bengal.

View on Google Maps
#06 Niagara of India & Coracle Rides

Dharmapuri

Hogenakkal Waterfalls
OVERVIEW District Information

World-famous for the thunderous Hogenakkal Falls on the Cauvery River, frequently termed the 'Niagara of India'. Visitors travel from across the globe to experience exhilarating round coracle rides amidst misty river gorges, natural herbal baths, and historic Adiyamans' heritage.

View on Google Maps
#07 Princess of Hills & Historic Fort

Dindigul

Kodaikanal & Rock Fort
OVERVIEW District Information

Crowned by the 'Princess of Hill Stations' Kodaikanal, famous for its star-shaped lake, pine forests, and misty Pillar Rocks. The plains feature the historic 17th-century Dindigul Rock Fort and the globally celebrated, aromatic Dindigul Thalappakatti biryani.

View on Google Maps
#08 Turmeric City & River Confluence

Erode

Bhavanisagar Dam & Sangameswarar
OVERVIEW District Information

Globally recognised as the 'Yellow City' for its massive turmeric production and textile trade. Home to Bhavanisagar Dam, one of the world's largest earthen dams, and the sacred Bhavani Sangameswarar Temple situated at the scenic confluence of the Cauvery, Bhavani, and Amudha rivers.

View on Google Maps
#09 Untouched Green Trails

Kallakurichi

Kalrayan Hills & Megam Falls
OVERVIEW District Information

Nestled around the pristine Kalrayan Hills of the Eastern Ghats, Kallakurichi offers dense forest trails, tranquil tribal hamlets, and refreshing cascades such as Megam Falls and Periyar Falls. An idyllic eco-tourism retreat away from commercial crowds.

View on Google Maps
#10 City of Thousand Temples

Kancheepuram

Kailasanathar Temple & Silk Sarees
OVERVIEW District Information

Revered as the 'City of Thousand Temples', Kancheepuram was the glorious capital of the Pallava dynasty. Architectural gems like Kailasanathar and Ekambareswarar temples stand alongside centuries-old handloom workshops crafting world-famous pure mulberry silk sarees with golden zari.

View on Google Maps
#11 Land's End & Triveni Sangam

Kanyakumari

Vivekananda Rock & Thiruvalluvar Statue
OVERVIEW District Information

The southern tip of the Indian mainland where the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean merge. Famous for breathtaking simultaneous sunrises and sunsets, the monolithic 133-foot Thiruvalluvar Statue, and the sacred Vivekananda Rock Memorial situated amidst the waves.

View on Google Maps
#12 Sangam Chera Capital & Home Textiles

Karur

Pasupatheeswarar & Amaravathi River
OVERVIEW District Information

Located on the banks of Amaravathi River, Karur was an illustrious capital and minting center of the Sangam Chera dynasty. Today, it is recognized internationally as India's leading home textile export hub, centered around the 7th-century Pasupatheeswarar Temple.

View on Google Maps
#13 Mango Capital of Tamil Nadu

Krishnagiri

Krishnagiri Dam & Fort
OVERVIEW District Information

Celebrated as the 'Mango Capital of Tamil Nadu', cultivating luscious mango varieties across expansive orchards. Its picturesque landscape is dotted with granite hillocks, the historic Krishnagiri hilltop fortress constructed by Vijayanagara rulers, and the scenic Krishnagiri Dam reservoir.

View on Google Maps
#14 Cultural Capital & City that Never Sleeps

Madurai

Meenakshi Temple & Nayakkar Mahal
OVERVIEW District Information

One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, Madurai is the soul of Tamil culture and the historic home of the Tamil Sangams. Renowned for the soaring gopurams of Meenakshi Amman Temple, the Indo-Saracenic Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal, fragrant Madurai Malli jasmine, and night street-food life.

View on Google Maps
#15 Cauvery Delta & Silappathikaram Heritage

Mayiladuthurai

Mayuranathaswamy & Poompuhar Beach
OVERVIEW District Information

Carved out of the fertile Cauvery delta, Mayiladuthurai boasts an exceptional density of ancient Chola temple architecture, including the Mayuranathaswamy Temple. Nearby lies Poompuhar, the celebrated ancient port city of the early Cholas immortalised in the epic Silappathikaram.

View on Google Maps
#16 Interfaith Pilgrim Shore & Sanctuary

Nagapattinam

Velankanni Basilica & Nagore Dargah
OVERVIEW District Information

A coastal district celebrated across the world as a beacon of interfaith communal harmony. Thousands of pilgrims visit the revered Basilica of Our Lady of Good Health at Velankanni, the centuries-old Nagore Dargah, and the serene coastal bird sanctuary at Point Calimere (Kodiakkarai).

View on Google Maps
#17 Egg City, Monolithic Fort & Hills

Namakkal

Namakkal Fort & Kolli Hills
OVERVIEW District Information

Famous for its massive single-stone hill fortress carved out of a monolithic rock, housing rock-cut cave temples of Lord Narasimha and the towering 18-foot open-air Anjaneyar statue. The district also encompasses the winding 70 hairpin bends of the mystical Kolli Hills.

View on Google Maps
#18 Queen of Hill Stations

Nilgiris

Ooty Toy Train & Tea Estates
OVERVIEW District Information

Revered as the 'Blue Mountains' and crowned by Ooty (Udhagamandalam), the UNESCO-listed Nilgiri Mountain Railway operates its iconic steam locomotives across dramatic ravines. Characterised by rolling emerald tea plantations, botanical gardens, and the indigenous culture of the Toda tribes.

View on Google Maps
#19 Historic Citadel & Agriculture

Perambalur

Ranjankudi Fort
OVERVIEW District Information

Steeped in 17th-century Carnatic military history, Perambalur is anchored by the well-preserved stone bastions and moats of Ranjankudi Fort, scene of historic Anglo-French clashes. The district is also an agricultural hub known for extensive shallot (small onion) and maize cultivation.

View on Google Maps
#20 Ancient Frescoes & Princely Heritage

Pudukkottai

Sittanavasal Cave Paintings
OVERVIEW District Information

A former princely state brimming with archaeological treasures. World-renowned for the 2nd-century BC Jain rock-cut cave temple at Sittannavasal containing extraordinary lotus pond frescoes, along with the magnificent rock-hewn Thirumayam fortress and Kudumiyanmalai musical inscriptions.

View on Google Maps
#21 Oceanic Engineering & Sacred Shores

Ramanathapuram

Pamban Sea Bridge & Rameswaram
OVERVIEW District Information

Sacred island terrain connected to the mainland by India's legendary sea engineering marvel—the Pamban Rail Bridge. Houses the magnificent Ramanathaswamy Temple featuring the longest carved stone temple corridor in the world, alongside the windswept coastal ghost town of Dhanushkodi.

View on Google Maps
#22 Nawab Heritage & Industrial Hub

Ranipet

Historic Delhi Gate Arcot
OVERVIEW District Information

Formed along the fertile Palar river basin, Ranipet carries the heritage of the Nawabs of Arcot, highlighted by the historic 18th-century Delhi Gate and royal fortifications. Today, it stands as one of India's preeminent leather tanning and industrial manufacturing zones.

View on Google Maps
#23 Jewel of South & Steel City

Salem

Yercaud Emerald Lake & Mettur Dam
OVERVIEW District Information

Surrounded by natural mineral reserves and high-grade steel manufacturing, Salem is guarded by the serene Shevaroy Hills. Features the tranquil hill retreat of Yercaud—'The Jewel of the South'—and the colossal Stanley Reservoir at Mettur Dam that irrigates the Cauvery delta.

View on Google Maps
#24 Chettinad Architecture & Spice Cuisine

Sivaganga

Chettinad Heritage Mansions
OVERVIEW District Information

Globally celebrated for its Chettinad heritage—monumental palatial mansions crafted with Burmese teak, Italian marble, and handcrafted Athangudi floor tiles. The district is equally renowned for the fiercely aromatic Chettinad cuisine and the sacred Pillayarpatti rock-cut Karpaga Vinayagar Temple.

View on Google Maps
#25 Spa of South India & Cascades

Tenkasi

Courtallam Falls & Kasi Viswanathar
OVERVIEW District Information

Nestled beneath the Agasthiyar hills of the Western Ghats, Tenkasi is internationally christened the 'Spa of South India' for Courtallam's medicinal herbal waterfalls. The historic town is presided over by the soaring 180-foot gopuram of the Kasi Viswanathar Temple.

View on Google Maps
#26 Rice Bowl of Tamil Nadu & Chola Wonder

Thanjavur

Brihadisvara Big Temple
OVERVIEW District Information

The undisputed cultural and agricultural heartland of Tamil Nadu, celebrated as the 'Rice Bowl of Tamil Nadu'. Home to the monumental 11th-century Brihadeeswarar Temple (Big Temple) built by Raja Raja Chola I—a UNESCO masterwork of granite architecture—along with Chola bronze casting and classical Carnatic music.

View on Google Maps
#27 Highwavys Mist & Plantation Valleys

Theni

Meghamalai Hills & Suruli Falls
OVERVIEW District Information

Cradled in a breathtaking valley flanked by the Western Ghats, Theni is renowned for the emerald peaks of Meghamalai (Highwavys), fragrant cardamom estates, and cascading Suruli Falls celebrated in Sangam poetry. An essential gateway for nature trekking and plantation tours.

View on Google Maps
#28 Pearl City & Seashore Murugan Shrine

Thoothukudi

Thiruchendur Murugan Temple & Port
OVERVIEW District Information

Historically celebrated as the 'Pearl City' for its centuries-old deep-sea pearl fishery and major international seaport. Home to the seashore pilgrimage destination of Thiruchendur Subramanya Swamy Temple, the Basilica of Our Lady of Snows, and delicious Tuticorin macroon confectionery.

View on Google Maps
#29 Citadel on Ancient Rock & Srirangam

Tiruchirappalli

Rockfort & Srirangam Temple
OVERVIEW District Information

Centered around the Cauvery river, Tiruchirappalli is dominated by the dramatic 3.8-billion-year-old monolithic Rockfort hosting the Ucchi Pillayar Temple. Across the river stands Srirangam Sri Ranganathaswamy Temple, the largest functioning Hindu temple complex in the world, alongside the historic 2nd-century Kallanai Grand Anicut.

View on Google Maps
#30 Land of Halwa & Musical Pillars

Tirunelveli

Nellaiappar Temple & Tamirabarani
OVERVIEW District Information

Situated along the sacred, perennial Tamirabarani River, Tirunelveli is anchored by the sprawling thousand-year-old Nellaiappar Temple celebrated for its musical stone pillars. Renowned worldwide for mouth-watering wheat halwa prepared with ghee and pure river water.

View on Google Maps
#31 Yelagiri Hill Station & Rose Gardens

Tirupathur

Yelagiri Hills & Jalagamparai Falls
OVERVIEW District Information

Bordering the Javadi and Yelagiri hill ranges, Tirupathur offers scenic trekking across peaceful Yelagiri hills with its rose gardens, paragliding camps, and Punganoor lake. Also famous for the roaring seasonal Jalagamparai Waterfalls and historic trading routes.

View on Google Maps
#32 Knitwear Capital of India

Tiruppur

Thirumoorthy Dam & Avinashi
OVERVIEW District Information

Internationally acclaimed as the 'Knitwear Capital of India', driving global garment exports with unmatched industrial dynamism. The scenic countryside is enriched by Thirumoorthy Dam and Falls near Udumalpet, with panoramic views of the Western Ghats and the historic Avinashilingeswarar Temple.

View on Google Maps
#33 Arupadaiveedu Hill Shrine

Tiruvallur

Tiruttani Murugan Temple
OVERVIEW District Information

Sacred frontier district hosting the revered hill shrine of Tiruttani, the fifth abode among Lord Murugan's Arupadaiveedu, where the deity found tranquility. Also renowned for the Sri Veeraraghava Swamy Temple and the life-sustaining Poondi and Chembarambakkam freshwater reservoirs.

View on Google Maps
#34 Agni Sthalam & Arunachala Mountain

Tiruvannamalai

Arunachaleswarar Temple & Girivalam
OVERVIEW District Information

One of the Pancha Bhoota Sthalas representing the element Fire (Agni), dominated by the sacred mountain of Arunachala and the imposing Annamalaiyar Temple. Millions of devotees undertake the 14-km barefoot circumambulation (Girivalam) around the hill, alongside the world-famous spiritual sanctuary of Sri Ramana Maharshi Ashram.

View on Google Maps
#35 Carnatic Music Trinity & Grand Chariot

Tiruvarur

Thyagaraja Temple & Aazhi Ther
OVERVIEW District Information

Renowned as the historic birthplace of the Musical Trinity of Carnatic music—Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, and Syama Sastri. Dominated by the magnificent Sri Thyagaraja Temple, boasting the world's largest temple chariot—the legendary multi-story 'Aazhi Ther'—and the expansive Kamalalayam temple tank.

View on Google Maps
#36 16th-Century Moated Citadel & Golden Shrine

Vellore

Vellore Fort & Jalakanteswarar
OVERVIEW District Information

Anchored by the impenetrable 16th-century Vellore Fort surrounded by a wide water moat, which hosted the historic Vellore Sepoy Mutiny of 1806. The fort encloses the intricately sculpted Jalakanteswarar Temple, while nearby Sripuram features the dazzling Mahalakshmi Golden Temple.

View on Google Maps
#37 Troy of the East & Granite Bastions

Viluppuram

Gingee Fort (Troy of the East)
OVERVIEW District Information

Crowned by the legendary Gingee Fort, acclaimed by the British as the 'Troy of the East' for its impregnable hill citadels spread across three towering crags (Rajagiri, Krishnagiri, and Chandrayandurg). The district also preserves rich Sangam archaeological sites and sacred rural folk heritage.

View on Google Maps
#38 Official Emblem of Tamil Nadu & Palkova

Virudhunagar

Srivilliputhur Andal Temple Tower
OVERVIEW District Information

Celebrated for the iconic 11-tiered, 192-foot Rajagopuram of the Srivilliputhur Andal Temple, proudly immortalized as the official emblem of the Government of Tamil Nadu. The district is also universally famous for authentic Srivilliputhur Palkova sweet, crisp Virudhunagar layered parottas, and trading vigor.

Traditional Tamil Cuisine

Relish the authentic culinary traditions spiced with curry leaves, mustard seeds, and coconut
Breakfast Classic

Idli & Medu Vada

Steamed Rice Cakes & Crispy Lentil Fritters

Steamed, pillowy soft rice cakes (malli-poo idli) and crispy golden lentil vadas, served with hot sambar and freshly ground coconut and tomato chutneys.

Golden Crepe

Crispy Ghee Roast Dosa

Aromatic Pure Ghee Fermented Crepe

Wafer-thin, crispy fermented rice and black gram crepe roasted in fragrant pure ghee, stuffed with spiced potato masala and served with variety chutneys.

Grand Traditional Feast

Banana Leaf Meals (Saapadu)

Traditional South Indian Plantain Leaf Banquet

The authentic Tamil feast served on a fresh plantain leaf with steamed Ponni rice, ghee paruppu, sambar, pepper rasam, kootu, poriyal, appalam, and buttermilk.

Spicy Heritage

Chettinad Pepper Chicken

Authentic Karaikudi Pepper Masala Roast

World-renowned Chettinad non-veg delicacy cooked with freshly hand-roasted black peppercorns, shallots, curry leaves, and traditional stone flower (kalpasi).

Royal Dum Biryani

Dindigul Seeraga Samba Biryani

Fragrant Small-Grain Dum Biryani

Iconic Tamil Nadu dum biryani cooked with tiny, fragrant Seeraga Samba rice, tender meat, and traditional whole spices, served with sour brinjal dalcha.

Street Food Legend

Madurai Bun Parotta & Salna

Flaky Layered Bread with Spicy Gravy

Flaky, multi-layered golden parotta crisp on the outside and soft inside, served hot with rich, spicy roadside chicken gravy (empty salna).

Kaapi Culture

Kumbakonam Degree Filter Coffee

Aromatic Fresh Chicory Brew in Brass Dabara

Piping hot, frothy South Indian filter coffee brewed from freshly roasted chicory beans and thick cow's milk, served in the traditional brass dabara and tumbler.

Royal Cold Dessert

Madurai Famous Jigarthanda

Cooling Almond Gum & Reduced Milk Drink

Historic cooling royal dessert beverage layered with cooling almond gum (badam pisin), nannari syrup, boiled reduced basundi milk, and rich malai ice cream.
